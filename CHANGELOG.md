# Changelog

All notable changes to Pursuit are recorded here.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-11-02

### Added

- Private calendar feed for scheduled follow-ups.
- Calendar events include the company, role, application link, and a 15-minute reminder.
- Follow-ups with a recorded reply, or applications that are closed, are excluded automatically.
- Regenerate control for revoking a leaked or unwanted feed URL.

### Changed

- Company rows now use company logos instead of contact photos.
- The landing header is reduced to the Pursuit wordmark.
- Login and signup screens now share the landing page pastel, typography, and form language.
- The halftone auth artwork was removed.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-01

### Added

- Public landing page with a live product preview.
- Direct demo entry point.
- Public privacy and terms pages.

### Changed

- Replaced the initial dashboard preview with a richer demo workspace image.
- Strengthened the public README with architecture, setup, testing, and deployment notes.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-31

### Added

- Initial public beta of Pursuit.
- Google verified account creation and onboarding.
- Application tracking, email history, follow-ups, templates, companies, analytics, and CSV export.
- Read-only demo workspace.
- Six-step product tour.
